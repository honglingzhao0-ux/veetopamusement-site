# VEETOP Catalogue 2026: 210 Models Across Six Product Lines

Our 2026 catalogue is now available online in full, with 210 models across six product lines. Every model in the range is manufactured in our own factory, CE certified, and available with OEM/ODM customization.

## What is in the range

**Arcade video games and simulators** is our largest line at 58 models, covering full-size racing and moto simulators, wooden-cabinet upright games, and the redemption-style arcade cabinets that perform well in mall units. The simulator formats in this line are the highest revenue per square metre in any venue, and they are the category we see new operators buying first.

**Redemption and ticket games** covers 60 models of prize-dispensing games - the ticket economy workhorses. This is the line where prize cost and claw tuning determine whether the venue's margin holds, so it is worth understanding the economics before ordering. Our [guide to claw crane economics](/blog/claw-crane-cost-and-payback-guide/) covers the numbers in detail.

**Sports and skill games** includes 45 models, from two-player air hockey and magic hockey tables to the interactive sports simulators that carry strong appeal in family venues. Two-player formats are worth prioritising because they double the revenue from a single machine footprint.

**Claw cranes** covers 22 models in three distinct formats: compact mini cranes for low-footfall positions, standard single cabinets, and the wider twin and four-player units for high-traffic mall locations. Cabinets are supplied with adjustable claw strength so operators can tune them to their own prize mix and traffic.

**Kiddie rides** has 15 models, from the two-seat 3D horse and car units through to six-player luxury carousels. Kiddie rides extend family dwell time, which is one of the strongest levers on food and drink revenue in a mixed venue.

**Prize merchandisers** has 10 models, including key master and push machines that dispense a prize directly for a coin. These are the highest hit-rate machines with young children and the lowest capital cost per unit in the range.

## Ordering, customization and shipping

We manufacture rather than trade, which means the customization path is direct. Logo and artwork application, cabinet colour, control panel configuration, and software or language settings are all handled in-house. For OEM/ODM projects we can develop to a customer specification rather than adapting an existing model.

Typical order process:

1. Send the model numbers, quantities, destination port and country
2. We confirm price, lead time, container loading plan and shipping terms in the quotation
3. Production against the confirmed specification, with progress photos
4. Balance against shipping documents, then dispatch

All machines are CE certified for the European market. We export to more than 150 countries and can advise on the certification route required for your destination.
